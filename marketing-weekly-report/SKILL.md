description: Weekly marketing report for the CEO. Use every Friday or on request.
---
## Steps
1. Gather: posts published and performance, site traffic, leads from
   suppliers and buyers, newsletter growth, outreach sent/replied
   (from the tracker), event progress.
2. Summarise: wins, misses, learnings, next week's plan, spend vs budget,
   decisions needed.
3. State sample sizes and uncertainty honestly.
4. Save to Drive and post a short summary in Slack for the CEO.
