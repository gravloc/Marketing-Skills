## Steps
1. Research dates, location, ticket and booth costs, attendee profile and
   speaker/startup opportunities (browser, official sites).
2. Compare options by cost, audience fit and timing; recommend the cheapest
   way to reach target engineers (attend vs booth vs side meetings).
3. Hand the budget to the CFO agent via the founder. No bookings or payments.
4. Prep: target meeting list, demo plan, collateral, follow-up sequence.
5. After the event: leads logged, follow-ups drafted, lessons added to MEMORY.md.
