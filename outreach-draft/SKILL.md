description: Draft personalised outreach messages or emails to suppliers, engineers or partners. Use with the Business Developer agent for founding-supplier outreach.
---
## Steps
1. Research the person and company from public sources only (browser).
   Note one real, specific reason to contact them.
2. Draft a short message (under 120 words): who we are, why them, the offer
   (only terms approved by the founder), a low-pressure ask.
3. Make 2 variants (formal / conversational) and a follow-up for day 7.
4. Include opt-out wording for email. Respect anti-spam rules and
   LinkedIn's limits; no bulk automation.
5. Create Gmail drafts only. Never send. Log the lead in the tracker Sheet.
6. Do not make promises about pricing, fees, timelines or partnerships.
