name: competitor-watch
description: Monitor competitors such as satsearch and satbase and summarise changes. Use for monthly competitor updates or when positioning is questioned.
---
## Steps
1. Browse public pages only: features, categories, pricing if public,
   blog, announcements. No logging in, no scraping personal data.
2. Record facts with dates and source URLs. Mark opinion separately.
3. Summarise: what changed, what it means for GRAVLOC, positioning
   suggestions. Never disparage them in public copy.
4. Update the competitor sheet and tell the CEO about anything material.
