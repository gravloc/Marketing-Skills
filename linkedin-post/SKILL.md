## Steps
1. Pick the pillar and audience. Write 3 hook options.
2. Draft 120-250 words: one idea, one concrete detail, one question or CTA.
3. No hype words, no unproven claims, no tagging real people or companies
   without approval.
4. Run the claims-check skill, then give the draft to the founder.
5. Never post. Hand over copy plus suggested time and any image brief.
