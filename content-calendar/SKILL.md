## Steps
1. Read MEMORY.md for goals, pillars and claim limits.
2. Plan 4 weeks in Sheets: date, channel, pillar, topic, goal, owner,
   status (idea / drafted / approved / published).
3. Balance: supplier-facing and buyer-facing content, education over promotion.
4. Tie each piece to a metric.
5. Present the plan for founder approval before scheduling anything.
